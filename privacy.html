Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

In short: Document Studio does not collect, store or send any of your personal data or documents. Everything you create or open stays on your own computer.

Who we are

Document Studio is a free Windows app made by Yash Deherkar.

What the app does with your information

  • Your documents and files are opened, edited and saved only on your computer, in the places you choose. They are never uploaded.
  • Text recognition (OCR) on scanned pages runs entirely on your computer.
  • Settings, recent files and drafts are kept in the app's own storage on your computer so it can restore your work.
  • Automatic backups, if you use them, are saved in your Documents folder.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to share.

Internet connections

The app works offline. When an internet connection is available it may download its display fonts (Fraunces, Inter and IBM Plex Mono) from Google Fonts, as any web page would; this sends no document content. Converting Word, Excel or PowerPoint files uses Microsoft Office installed on your own computer, if present.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use cookies or analytics. It remembers the colour you pick only in your own browser. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
