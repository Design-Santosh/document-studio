What it does

One app instead of five.

The everyday paperwork tools, made to feel good to use — and to look good when you share the result.

Documents

Start from a template that already looks finished.

Reports, letters, invoices, meeting minutes, CVs, proposals, manuals, test reports and more. Type into it and it stays tidy: headings, tables, charts and page numbers sort themselves out.

  • 14 ready-made templates, plus a blank page

Edit PDF

Change the words already in a PDF.

Open any PDF and fix a typo in the original text — it keeps the same font and position. Add text, highlights, boxes, pictures and your signature, fill forms, and reorder pages by dragging.

  • Recognise text on scanned pages (OCR), offline
  • Turn a PDF into an editable document
  • Merge PDFs and add blank pages

Gantt charts

Plan a project in minutes.

Type tasks into the table or paste them from Excel. Drag bars to move them, drag an end to change the length — later tasks follow along. Phases, milestones, owners and progress are all there.

  • Days, weeks or months · working days only
  • Export as a picture or PDF
  • Drop the chart straight into a document

  • Export as PNG, SVG or PDF

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no ads and no sign-up.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything — including text recognition on scanned PDFs and flowchart drawing — runs on your PC. Your documents are never uploaded anywhere.

Do I need Microsoft Office?

Not for most things. Creating documents, editing PDFs, OCR, Gantt charts, flowcharts and exporting to PDF, Word, Markdown or pictures all work without it.

Converting Word, Excel or PowerPoint files, and “Edit as document” for PDFs, use Microsoft Office if it's installed on your PC.

Which Windows versions does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning for apps that aren't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway to continue.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Document Studio → Uninstall. Your documents are kept.
